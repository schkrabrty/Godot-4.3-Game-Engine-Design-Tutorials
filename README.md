# Godot-4.3-Game-Engine-Design-Tutorials

In this tutorial, I will show you how can you create your own custom shaders, and play with them in your game using your own custom logic and physics. In the video below, I will show you how can you write shaders in Godot to simulate a wind effect on grasses and a ball (while floating on the water). I will also show you how to create a water shader. Next, I will show you how can you consider the directional light direction and its color in your fragment shaders. Finally, I will show you how can you use Physics with your custom shaders so that your shaders can be used with physics in normal games in Godot. 

[![A video to showcase the effects of shaders and interaction with them using Physics in Godot](/Images%20for%20Readme/Shader_Example_Image.png)](https://youtu.be/Qj4u-TKb4J8)

In the video below, I will showcase some examples of Cel-shading, Dissolving effect using shaders, and Parallax Occlusion Mapping using Gdshader in Godot. 

[![A video to showcase the effects of Cel-shading, Dissolving Effect, and Parallax Occlusion Mapping in Godot](/Images%20for%20Readme/Other_Shaders_from_Assignment_4_Thumbnail.png)](https://youtu.be/qy8PbZjIpUc)

I hope, this tutorial will help you to understand the concepts better. Feel free to play with the tutorial to understand the concepts better.

Please check [this tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x1TwfpQRhbk) out to understand the **Parallax Occlusion Mapping Shader**. I have also used the same [Bricks asset](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bricks082A) in my tutorial.
